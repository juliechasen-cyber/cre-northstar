# Email Drip Sequence

The follow-up campaign nobody writes. The investor-email output is touch
zero's polished sibling; this sequence carries the listing from launch to
the offer deadline. Each email must stand alone — a reader who ignored the
first three still gets the full picture from the fourth.

## The touches

1. **Day 0 — launch.** The announcement. Property, headline numbers, ask.
   Closes with the OM offer.
2. **Day 4–6 — "the OM is moving."** Short. One fact the launch email
   didn't lead with (unit mix, loss-to-lease, submarket comp). Social
   proof posture without invented claims — "the OM is in circulation" is
   fine; "strong interest" requires a fact.
3. **Day 10–14 — tour / call nudge.** One question email: are they
   underwriting it, and what would they need to see? Offer tour dates or
   a 15-minute call.
4. **Offers due minus 5–7 days — last call.** Deadline front and center,
   subject line included. Recap the two strongest numbers. This is the
   shortest email of the four.

If deal-facts has an offer deadline, put real dates in `send` and pace
touches 2–4 backward from it. No deadline → day offsets ("Day 10").
If the window is too tight for four touches, drop touch 2, never touch 4.

## Rules

- Same grounding as everything: every number from deal-facts only.
- Subjects ≤ 55 chars, natural phrasing, no separator characters
  (deliverability). Two options per email.
- Bodies 60–140 words, shrinking as the sequence progresses. Line breaks
  between ideas; no wall of text.
- First person broker voice. `{FIRST_NAME}` greeting token.
- No urgency theater ("last chance!!", countdown emoji). The deadline is
  the urgency; state it plainly.
