# LinkedIn Announcement

The listing announcement a broker posts from their own profile. It performs
two jobs: market this deal, and make the broker look institutional.

## Structure

- **Hook line** (no "🚨 JUST LISTED 🚨") — property type, unit count,
  submarket, and the single most compelling approved number.
- **Body** — 3–5 short lines: the 2–3 strongest facts (price/unit, cap
  rate, occupancy, loss-to-lease upside). One line per fact. Line breaks
  between every line — LinkedIn collapses paragraphs.
- **Close** — offer process/deadline + "DM me or reach out at {email}".
- **Hashtags** — 3–5, specific over generic: #CRE #Multifamily +
  #{City}RealEstate. No #blessed-tier tags.

## Rules

- ≤ 1,100 characters (safely under LinkedIn's 1,300 "see more" fold for
  the load-bearing content — hook and first fact must land before the fold).
- Suggested image: `media.heroPhotoId` from deal-facts. Never suggest text-heavy
  images (floorplans, document screenshots).
- Written in the broker's first person ("I'm pleased to bring to
  market…"), not CRE Northstar's.
- No emoji, per brand voice. If the broker wants emoji they can add their
  own — the default is restraint.
