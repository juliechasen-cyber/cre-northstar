# Sales Script

The phone script a broker uses to pitch this listing to an investor.
Modeled on institutional brokerage call practice (the Marcus & Millichap /
CBRE / Newmark register): state the reason for the call immediately, frame
everything around the investor's return, let the numbers persuade, and
aim the whole call at one outcome — send the OM and book a 15-minute
meeting. The phone call is never the close.

## Structure (sections, in this order)

- **Opening — 15 seconds** — name, firm, reason for the call in one
  breath. No "how are you today"; you are interrupting their day, so
  earn the interruption: "The purpose of my call is…". End with a
  permission check ("Did I catch you at a workable moment?").
- **The hook** — property type, unit count, submarket, and the single
  most compelling approved number. One or two sentences.
- **Value stack** — the 2–3 strongest facts, one spoken line each
  (price/unit, cap rate, occupancy, loss-to-lease upside). Say the
  number, then what it means for the buyer.
- **Qualifying questions** — two questions that surface fit: are they
  actively acquiring in this market, and what they're solving for
  (yield, scale, 1031 timing).
- **Close** — the concrete next step: "I'll send the OM today — do you
  have 15 minutes Thursday to walk the numbers?" Tie to the offer
  deadline when there is one.

## Objection handling (the `objections` array)

Cover the three a broker will actually hear, in spoken voice:

1. **"Just send me the OM."** — agree, then keep the meeting alive:
   send it today + one reason a 15-minute walkthrough pays for itself.
2. **"The price seems high."** / pricing pushback — anchor to an
   approved metric (price/unit vs. the market, in-place vs. market
   rents); if no supporting fact exists, concede gracefully and pivot
   to the process ("the market will set it — that's what the call
   process is for").
3. **"We're not buying right now."** — qualify the timeline, offer the
   OM for their file, ask who in their network is active.

## Voicemail

≤ 30 seconds spoken: name, firm, the hook number, one sentence of value,
callback + "I'll also email the summary." Nothing that requires a
callback to make sense.

## Rules

- Spoken register: contractions are fine here (the one output where the
  no-contractions rule does not apply — people don't talk in wire copy).
  Short sentences. Read every line aloud before returning it.
- First person, the broker's voice — never CRE Northstar's.
- Every number from deal-facts only. The broker's own credibility line
  (track record, recent closings) is a bracketed slot —
  `[YOUR RECENT SALE OR ACTIVE LISTING IN THIS SUBMARKET]` — never
  invented.
- No hype vocabulary (brand voice §: banned list applies to scripts
  too). Confidence comes from the numbers and the named submarket.
- Coach notes (`coach`) are stage directions, ≤ 15 words: pace, tone,
  when to pause. Not content.
- If the deal is thin (few approved facts), write a shorter script —
  never pad the value stack with adjectives.
