# Listing Site Copy

Every deal gets posted to LoopNet and Crexi, and their forms want the same
things: a headline, a description, highlight bullets, and key facts. This
output is the paste-ready block — the broker fills the form in two minutes
instead of retyping the OM.

## Fields

- **Headline** — ≤ 100 chars. Property type + unit count + submarket +
  the hook number. Marketplace search skims headlines; front-load the
  searchable terms ("48-Unit Multifamily — North Park, San Diego —
  $10.9M").
- **Summary / teaser** — 1–2 sentences for the search-results card. The
  single reason to click.
- **Description** — 2–3 paragraphs. Opportunity first, then property
  specifics, then submarket. This is marketplace copy, not the OM:
  shorter sentences than the executive memo, and every claim still
  sourced.
- **Highlights** — 4–6 bullets, ≤ 12 words each. Numbers over adjectives.
- **Key facts** — the label/value rows the marketplaces show in their
  facts panel: price, units, building SF, year built, cap rate,
  occupancy, price/unit — whichever exist in deal-facts.

## Rules

- Grounding as always — deal-facts only, omit what isn't there.
- No "call for details" filler; if a fact is absent, silence beats a tease.
- Third person. The marketplaces read as neutral inventory, not the
  broker's voice.
- No contact block — the marketplace layouts inject the broker card
  themselves.
