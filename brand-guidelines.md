## 2. Color Palette

Seven official brand colors.

| Role | Name | Hex | RGB | CMYK | Usage |
|------|------|-----|-----|------|-------|
| Primary dark | Steel Navy | `#344754` | 52 / 71 / 84 | 81 / 63 / 49 / 35 | Headings, body text, dark grounds |
| Neutral dark | Charcoal | `#424143` | 66 / 65 / 67 | 68 / 62 / 58 / 45 | Secondary dark, footer, deep neutral |
| Accent (energy) | Lime | `#8cc040` | 140 / 192 / 64 | 51 / 3 / 99 / 0 | CTAs, highlights, "now" moments |
| Accent (calm) | Teal | `#87c1bf` | 135 / 193 / 191 | 47 / 8 / 26 / 0 | Secondary accent, chart series, tags |
| Neutral mid | Gray | `#bcbec0` | 188 / 190 / 192 | 26 / 20 / 20 / 0 | Dividers, borders, muted text |
| Neutral light | Mist | `#eaedf2` | 234 / 237 / 242 | 7 / 4 / 2 / 0 | Card grounds, soft section backgrounds |
| Ground | Off-white | `#f8f9fa` | 248 / 249 / 250 | 2 / 1 / 1 / 0 | Default page background |

**Quick rules:**
- Steel Navy on Off-white / Mist is the default combination.
- Lime is the energy color — CTAs and "now" moments only. Never decorative.
- Teal is the calm accent — good for secondary emphasis, chart series, hover states.
- Mist for card grounds; Off-white for default page ground.
- Charcoal for footer / very deep neutrals when Steel Navy would feel too blue.

## 3. Typography

Both fonts are universally installed on Windows, macOS, iOS, Android, and web — no @font-face needed.

| Role | Font | Weights | Notes |
|------|------|---------|-------|
| Heading / display | **Georgia** | 400, 700 | Serif — pairs with Calibri for editorial contrast, reads credible/professional |
| Body | **Calibri** | 400, 600, 700 | Sans-serif — clean, native to Office; primary paragraph text |
| Mono / labels (optional) | Consolas or system monospace | 400, 700 | For code, small caps labels, metadata |

**Quick rules:**
- Georgia for any headline, section title, hero name.
- Calibri for paragraphs, captions, body copy, UI text.
- Fall back stack for web: `Georgia, 'Times New Roman', serif` and `Calibri, 'Segoe UI', system-ui, sans-serif`.
- Uppercase small labels: Calibri 600 with letter-spacing.

## 4. Voice

**Sounds like:** Professional, credible, restrained. Institutional-grade — the voice of a firm that operates in commercial real estate at scale.

**Never:**
- Playful, casual, or joky. No puns, no wink-and-nudge asides, no informal internet phrasing.
- Unprofessional tone of any kind. Nothing that would feel out of place in a lender memo, an LP update, or a boardroom deck.
- Marketing hype ("game-changing," "revolutionary," "unlock").
- Emoji in external-facing content.

## 6. Visual Style

**Reference site:** [rockco.com](https://www.rockco.com) — look-and-feel target. Match its restraint, whitespace, and institutional polish.

**General:**
- Generous whitespace. Density is earned.
- Thin dividers (1px, Gray). Never heavy rules.
- No gradients, no drop shadows, no decorative embellishment.
- Buttons: rounded rectangles (not pills), Steel Navy fill with Off-white text; Lime fill for primary CTA only.

**Photography:**
- Two subjects only: **buildings and people.**
- **Buildings:** architectural / editorial. Urban and commercial assets. Real properties, not stock photography. Clean composition, natural light, neutral color grading.
- **People:** professional context — in-office, at properties, in meetings, on-site. Non-staged expressions. No stock-photo laughter, no lifestyle warmth theater.
- Overall grading: neutral, restrained. No heavy filters.

**Charts & data viz:**
- Series colors in order: **Steel Navy → Teal → Lime → Charcoal → Gray.** Lime reserved for the emphasized series or the "now" data point.
- Backgrounds: Off-white default; Mist for card-grounded charts. No gradients.
- Gridlines: 1px, Gray, horizontal only, sparse. Drop them entirely when the chart reads without.
- Axis labels: Calibri 400, Charcoal, small.
- Hero numbers (KPI tiles): Georgia 700, Steel Navy, big and confident. Supporting label in Calibri 600 small caps.
- No 3D, no shadows, no unnecessary decoration. Data-ink ratio high.
- Chart titles: Georgia 700; captions and sourcing: Calibri 400, Gray.

## 7. Logo & Mark

**Primary mark:** compass rose / north star logomark — a circular compass with a multi-point star at the center and a marked "N" for north at the top. Rendered in black on light grounds; reverse to Off-white on Steel Navy for dark grounds.

**File location:** `clients/cre-northstar/assets/logo/` — drop the source files (SVG preferred, plus PNG at 512px and 1024px) here.

**Usage:**
- Never rotate, skew, or distort.
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of the "N" glyph.
- Minimum size: 24px digital / 0.25" print.
- On dark grounds, invert to Off-white; do not use Lime or Teal as fill for the mark.

**Wordmark:** **NORTHSTAR** — Georgia 700, all caps, Steel Navy. Sits alongside the compass mark (mark left, wordmark right, vertically centered). "CRE" is the sector prefix used in longer-form contexts (email signatures, page headers, "CRE Northstar Insights," etc.) but is not part of the visual signature.
